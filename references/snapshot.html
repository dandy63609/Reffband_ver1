Help & Support

Can I create and share my own goshuin design?+

Yes. GoShuin’s generation feature lets you create an original digital goshuin design and share your finished creation.

What is the GoShuin app?+

GoShuin is a goshuin map and journal for iPhone by AE Studio. Find temples and shrines, record visits, and save goshuin photos with dates and notes. Basic browsing and local records need no account.

Can I use GoShuin in English?+

Yes—English, Japanese, Simplified Chinese and Traditional Chinese. Some place names, sources and photographed goshuin remain in Japanese.

Does GoShuin issue goshuin or replace my goshuincho?+

No. GoShuin saves photos and visit records. Receive goshuin at temples and shrines, follow their reception rules, and keep your physical goshuincho.

How do I save a goshuin page?+

Photograph or import a page, adjust the crop, and add a title, date and note. Recognition runs on your device. Confirm the suggested place, choose one manually, or save without a link. You can change the link later without deleting the scan.

What is free, and what is optional?+

The three starter illustrations need no account or purchase. Other available illustrations can be revealed with a scan linked to their exact place or an optional one-time pack. Marking a place as visited does not reveal its artwork. Additional artwork downloads require an internet connection and Sign in with Apple. Only if you confirm a free unlock, a small stamp thumbnail is sent to our server and Gemini to check the match. Original images are not sent, and the delivery server does not save image bytes.

How do purchases, restores and refunds work?+

All Japan is US$19.99 in the US storefront; available prefecture packs are US$2.99 each. Current regions are Toyama, Nagano, Kyoto, Hyogo and Hiroshima. Apple displays your local price before purchase. These are one-time purchases; no new subscription is offered. To restore, use the same Apple Account and choose Restore Purchases in Settings. For refunds, use Apple’s purchase support.

Where are my records stored?+

Visits, notes, favourites and offering amounts stay on your device. Stamp text recognition also runs on device. Sign in with Apple to enable optional private iCloud sync for your scan library. Offering amounts are personal notes; the app does not transfer money. See the privacy policy for details.

How do I delete my account?+

Open Settings → Account, start deletion and reauthenticate with the relevant Apple account. Deletion clears your private cloud library, revokes Apple sign-in, and removes server-held account, device, download and submission history. Public place information and minimal anti-replay records remain. If deletion fails, your account is retained so you can check your connection and retry.

What should I check before visiting?+

Check the venue’s official opening hours, prices and goshuin availability. Follow its worship and photography rules. Coverage is partial; place data uses Wikidata (CC0) and OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL).

What are goshuin and a goshuincho?+

A goshuin is a seal with calligraphy received at many Japanese shrines and temples as a record of a visit for worship. A goshuincho is the book used to keep these pages. Practices vary by place. GoShuin organises photos of the stamps you receive; it does not issue religious seals. See the Japan National Tourism Organization’s goshuin guide for context.
